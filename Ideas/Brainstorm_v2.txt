1. Mini events should NOT have packages
For birthdays, baby showers, graduations, pool parties, grooves, overnight events, etc., I'd make the customer choose between:

Hourly coverage
- 1 hour
- 2 hours
- 3 hours
- 4+ hours

OR we can make it a

Deliverable-based
- X edited photos
- X short videos/reels
- X photos + X videos

This solves the "R5,500 for a birthday" problem immediately.

For example, the platform could calculate:

> 2 hours · Graduation · 80 edited photos · 2 short videos
> 850

Instead of making someone buy a luxury production package.

And importantly, we should not hardcode these prices. VSM admin must have pricing engine where we can change rates without redeploying.
